Technology

Physical AI that acts in the real world

AI controls real race cars, in real time, wheel-to-wheel at high speed. We test every software release on a real race car at up to 280 km/h before it goes into a race.

01 — PERCEPTION

See

Understanding the track, the car and every rival around it.

  • Computer vision
  • Sensor fusion: LiDAR, radar, cameras
02 — PLANNING

Decide

Choosing the fastest safe line, overtakes and defence, many times a second.

  • Machine learning
  • Optimised path planning and prediction
03 — CONTROL

Act

Turning decisions into steering, throttle and brake at the limit of grip.

  • Vehicle dynamics
  • Safety-critical control systems

Why it matters
If AI can operate safely at racing speeds, it can power the next generation of autonomy across industries.

Autonomous racing is the ultimate real-world benchmark for Physical AI. It validates perception, decision-making and control under pressure, at speed and in the face of risk. This is where AI moves beyond simulation and proves itself in reality.

Beyond racing

Racing is our testing ground for real-world deployment

The same stack that races at 280 km/h is built into products and services for motorsport, mobility and industry.

Racing Coach

A real-time AI driving coach: a lap-by-lap plan, racing-line corrections and throttle and brake deltas against the AI-optimal lap.

For racing schools, OEM driver programmes, sim racing and track days

ADAS & obstacle avoidance

High-speed collision avoidance for road and private-area vehicles, built on our racing perception and planning.
